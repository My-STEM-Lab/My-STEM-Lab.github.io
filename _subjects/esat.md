## The test

The ESAT is made up of five modules — Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
Most engineering and science courses require **Mathematics 1 plus two others**. I teach
**Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2 and Physics**.

Each module is **27 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes**, separately timed with no time carried
between them. It's computer-based at a Pearson VUE test centre, an erasable booklet is provided for
working, and **calculators are not allowed**.

Two facts drive everything about preparing for it:

1. **89 seconds per question.** The content is largely GCSE and AS level — the difficulty is entirely
   in the pace. Fluency matters more than depth.
2. **There is no negative marking.** No answer box should ever be left empty.

## What we work on

**The no-calculator toolkit.** Squares to 25², powers of 2, common surds to instant recall. Working in
fractions and surds until the last possible step. Estimating to one significant figure first, then
seeing which options survive — often only one does.

**Elimination over solution.** Substitute a convenient value. Set a variable to 0, 1 or infinity and
see what breaks. Check units and dimensions on every physics answer. Most ESAT questions can be cut
to two options without doing the actual mathematics.

**Three-pass timing.** Everything under a minute first, then the medium tier, then intelligent guesses
on what's left. A checkpoint at 20 minutes — you want 14 answered by then.

## How preparation usually runs

I typically run this as a **ten-week programme**: three hours a week, two on content and one on timed
practice, working directly from the official content specification so that coverage is provable rather
than assumed. Every timed set gets reviewed with each error classified — gap, too slow, slip, or misread —
and that classification drives the following week.

We can also run this as a student-led teaching, where the student gives the module they want to cover, and
what topic they are feeling stuck on, with **resources** being provided, **exclusively available** only if you tutor with me.
